## About the Book

### Genre
Dark comedy horror with satirical elements. Part of a 101-book series where each book features a different backpacker dying in a different hostel around the world.

### Tone
- Darkly humorous - death is treated with gallows humor
- Satirical - critiques travel/backpacker culture
- Horror - genuine suspense and graphic death sequences
- Literary accessible - vivid but not purple prose
