## What I'm Looking For

### Big Picture Feedback
Please pay attention to:
1. **Pacing** - Did the story move too fast/slow anywhere?
2. **Character** - Did the victim feel real? Did you care about them?
3. **Horror/Comedy Balance** - Did the dark humor work? Was it too much/little?
4. **The Death** - Was it satisfying? Surprising? Too obvious?
5. **Setting** - Could you visualize the hostel and city?

### Specific Questions
1. At what point (if any) did you get bored or want to skim?
2. Were there any parts that confused you?
3. Did the victim's fatal flaw feel believable?
4. Did you see the death coming? When?
5. Which character(s) did you like most? Least?
6. Was there anything that pulled you out of the story?
7. Did the ending satisfy you?

---

## How to Give Feedback

### Option 1: Inline Comments
If reading digitally, use comments/highlights for:
- Typos and errors
- Confusing sentences
- Questions as you read
- Reactions ("LOL", "OMG", "This is creepy")

### Option 2: Chapter-by-Chapter Notes
After each chapter, jot down:
- Your emotional state
- Any questions you have
- Anything that stood out (good or bad)

### Option 3: Summary Feedback
After finishing, complete the questionnaire at the end of this document.

---

## What I'm NOT Looking For

- Line editing (I have an editor for that)
- Rewriting suggestions
- Personal style preferences ("I would have written it differently")
- Comparison to other authors

Just tell me what works and what doesn't as a reader!
