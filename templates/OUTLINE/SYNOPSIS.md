## Medium Synopsis (500 words)

*For agent submissions, marketing materials*

### Act 1: Setup (125 words)
[Introduce protagonist in their ordinary world. Show their flaw or wound. Describe the inciting incident that disrupts their life. End with the decision that launches them into the story.]

### Act 2A: Rising Action (125 words)
[Protagonist pursues their goal. Initial obstacles appear. They meet allies and enemies. Things seem to be going okay, then a midpoint twist changes everything.]

### Act 2B: Complications (125 words)
[Stakes escalate. Protagonist's flaw causes problems. Allies may betray or fall. The antagonist gains ground. All seems lost at the dark night of the soul.]

### Act 3: Resolution (125 words)
[Protagonist has a revelation. They confront the antagonist. The climax unfolds. Conclude with resolution - how has the protagonist changed? What's the new normal?]
