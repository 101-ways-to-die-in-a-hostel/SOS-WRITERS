## Welcome Sequence

### Email 1: Welcome (Sent Immediately)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Welcome to [NEWSLETTER_NAME]! Here's your [FREEBIE]
- You're in! Let's start this adventure
- [FIRST_NAME], welcome to the family

**Body:**
```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

Welcome to [NEWSLETTER_NAME]! I'm so glad you're here.

As promised, here's your free [READER_MAGNET]:
[DOWNLOAD_LINK]

A little about me: [1-2 SENTENCES_ABOUT_YOU]

A little about what you'll get from me:
- [BENEFIT_1]
- [BENEFIT_2]
- [BENEFIT_3]

I'd love to know more about you! Hit reply and tell me: [QUESTION]

Talk soon,
[YOUR_NAME]

P.S. [TEASER_FOR_NEXT_EMAIL]
```

---

### Email 2: Story/Connection (Day 2-3)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Why I write [GENRE]
- The story behind [BOOK/SERIES]
- Here's something most people don't know about me

**Body:**
```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

[PERSONAL_STORY_THAT_CONNECTS_TO_YOUR_WRITING]

This is why I write what I write.

If you haven't started [BOOK_NAME] yet, here's the opening:

"[FIRST_PARAGRAPH_OR_HOOK]"

[LINK_TO_BUY/READ]

What draws you to [GENRE]? I'd love to hear.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

---

### Email 3: Value/Content (Day 5-7)

**Subject Line Options:**
- [X] books like [YOUR_BOOK] you might love
- My favorite [TOPIC] resources
- [HELPFUL_CONTENT_RELATED_TO_YOUR_GENRE]

**Body:**
```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

I wanted to share something I think you'll enjoy:

[VALUABLE_CONTENT - recommendations, tips, insights]

[DETAILS_AND_LINKS]

Let me know if you check any of these out!

[YOUR_NAME]

P.S. Quick reminder - if you haven't grabbed [BOOK_NAME] yet, it's available here: [LINK]
```

---

### Email 4: Social Proof (Day 10-14)

**Subject Line Options:**
- What readers are saying about [BOOK]
- "[QUOTE_FROM_REVIEW]"
- You're not alone if you felt [EMOTION] reading this

**Body:**
```
Hi [FIRST_NAME],

I wanted to share some messages I've received from readers like you:

"[REVIEW_1]" - [REVIEWER_NAME]

"[REVIEW_2]" - [REVIEWER_NAME]

"[REVIEW_3]" - [REVIEWER_NAME]

These messages mean everything to me.

If you've read [BOOK_NAME], I'd be incredibly grateful for a review. It helps other readers find the book.

[LINK_TO_REVIEW]

Thank you for being part of this community.

[YOUR_NAME]
```
