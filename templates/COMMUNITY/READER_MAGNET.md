## Reader Magnet Types

### Fiction Options

| Type | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|------|------|------|----------|
| Prequel Novella | Hooks into series, shows characters | Time to write | Series authors |
| Short Story | Quick to create, easy to consume | May not hook strongly | Any genre |
| First in Series | Strongest hook | Giving away a full book | Long series |
| Deleted Scene | Easy to create, exclusive | Requires existing book | Established authors |
| Alternate POV | Fresh perspective | Requires existing book | Character-driven books |
| Extended Epilogue | Fan service | Only for existing readers | Romance/series |
| Character Backstory | Deep worldbuilding | May spoil books | Fantasy/SciFi |

### Non-Fiction Options

| Type | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|------|------|------|----------|
| Companion Guide | High value, evergreen | Time to create | Complex worlds |
| World Map/Art | Visual appeal | Requires design | Fantasy/SciFi |
| Recipe Book | Unique angle | Genre-specific | Books with food elements |
| Playlist | Easy to create | Low perceived value | Any genre |
| Discussion Questions | Useful for book clubs | Niche appeal | Literary fiction |
